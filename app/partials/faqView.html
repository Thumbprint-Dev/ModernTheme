Finding what you need

How do I search for a product?

You can use the “Shop by Category” icons located on the home page to navigate to a product, or use the search box located on the navigation bar.

How do I know if something is in stock?

Inventory levels are located on the detail page of a product. Items that are out of stock cannot be purchased.

Can I favorite a product?

To favorite a product, click the Heart icon located in the upper right hand corner of the product thumbnail. To unfavorite a product, click the Heart icon again.

Can I request a product that isn't listed?

To request a product not listed on our site, please contact your sales rep or contact support@thumbprint.com.

After the order

How do I know if my order was submitted?

After each order is placed, an acknowledgement email is sent to the email address associated with your account.

How do I check my order status?

Click your name in the upper right hand corner of the navigation bar, and select Order History from the dropdown. If you cannot find the information, reach out to support@thumbprint.com.

Can I change or cancel my order?

In most circumstances, once an order is placed it cannot be cancelled or changed. To verify what the options are, reach out to support@thumbprint.com and they will do all they can for you.

What if something is missing, damaged, or wrong?

Contact support@thumbprint.com.

What's your return and refund policy?

Our return policy is located at Return Policy.

What payment methods do you accept?

Our payment terms are Credit Card only (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, or Discover).

Is my payment information secure?

Yes, your payment information is completely safe. Credit card payments are processed in strict compliance with PCI-DSS standards, which is the universal security benchmark for transmitting and protecting sensitive financial data during online transactions.

How is sales tax calculated?

Sales tax rates vary significantly by state, county, and city. We use a specialized, certified third-party tax integration to ensure accuracy.

Will I get a receipt or invoice for my order?

Receipts for orders can be found using the Order History button located in the navigation bar. Acknowledgement emails are sent to your email and also within your account information by selecting Notifications from the account dropdown in the navigation bar.

Shipping and delivery

How will I know that my order has shipped?

Shipment notifications are sent within 24 hours or sooner of your product shipping.

What are lead times for stocked vs. on-demand items?

All items that are stocked in any of our warehouses will ship no later than the next day – we strive for same-day shipments.

How much does shipping cost?

All packages are sent via UPS Ground and calculations for cost come directly from the carrier.

Can I track my order?

Once your package is shipped, the tracking number(s) will be sent to your email for your convenience.

Help & support

How do I contact support?

Visit our Contact Us page.

Who is my account rep?

Email support@thumbprint.com to learn who your account rep is.

What are your support hours?

Monday – Friday, 8am to 5pm ET. (Feel free to contact us outside of these hours and one of our team members will respond as soon as possible!)

How quickly will I get a response?

We strive to answer support questions within 4 business hours of receiving a request.
